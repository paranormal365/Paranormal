## Description

> IsHaunted is where paranormal investigators, the people who call them, and anyone curious about a
> haunted place meet. Version 1.1.4 is built for iPhone Duo. Fold it open in a dark room and the
> Paranormal Platform fills the inner screen: the magnetic field gauge, the sound level and the
> night's clock, with every control for the investigation in a column down the side. Mark a moment,
> log a cold spot or a touch, ask a question and wait for an answer while the phone types what you
> asked, change room, reset the base, switch on the flashlight. Fold it shut and it is a phone again,
> still recording.
>
> It arrives for Halloween. The same app books ghost walks, haunted dinners and investigation weekends,
> carries the guest's pass with no signal at the door, and shows the menus and the program for the
> night. Afterwards, sessions recorded at public haunted places can be published to the place, so the
> next visitor can play back what the last one recorded.
