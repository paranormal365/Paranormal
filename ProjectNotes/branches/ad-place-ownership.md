# The place ownership ad (10/22/2026)

Branch `feature/ad-place-ownership`, from develop (cc7fbbc3).

## The ask (Ben)

His new ad, "Advertisement Place Ownership.mp4", about claiming public venues and places. Make the
green screens show the claim on the site; the Declaration iPad was not a green screen and should be;
the doctor holds the phone and never taps, so it shows the owned result ("you own the Hospital Operating
Room"); the same tune in every scene, in the instruments and style of its time or place, real
instruments; ambient sound where a scene has none; the surgery fades out with the music into a new
version of the usual ending, the new icon built piece by piece, IsHaunted.com in the site's font and a
funny tagline. Then: make the name tag read "John Hancock" (it said "Handook").

## What was built

docs/ads/place-ownership/ — see its README for the scene table and the pipeline. In short:

- Ten screens tracked and replaced with the venue-claim flow (place page → Claim → claimed →
  Congratulations), drawn in the site's dark theme with the new icon; hands, gloves, claw and paws stay
  in front; the green spill is relit violet.
- The 1776 iPad keyed on its mint page and replaced like the others.
- "John Hancock." on the name tag, through the dissolve.
- The surgery phone: "Congratulations! You own Operating Room 3 now.", with a push-in so it can be read,
  then a fade into the ending.
- The ending builds the 10/15 icon (tile, arcs, windows one by one, the ghost, then its face), the
  wordmark in Irish Grover, "Claim it before the ghosts do." / "Find the place on IsHaunted.com and tap
  Claim it."
- One tune, ten arrangements and a finale, all real recordings; ambience for the three silent scenes.
