# EVP bench, first run — 2026-09-15

Item 242 phase 1. Produced by `tools/EvpLab` (`generate`, then `score`), corpus seed 242, Whisper small (`ggml-small.bin`,
SHA-1 55356645…, matches whisper.cpp's published hash), CPU only. The raw report follows the findings.

## What it found

1. **Whisper did not invent words from steady noise.** 0 of 160 pure-noise windows, 0 of 295 noise candidates from
   the detector's queue, 0 of 55 knocks/footsteps/creaks/thumps. Feared failure did not appear *on synthetic noise*;
   real tape has more structure (voices through walls, radios, mechanical rhythm), so this must be re-run on Ben's
   recordings before it is believed.
2. **Whisper does invent words from sounds shaped like speech that contain no words.** The same voice clips played
   backwards came back as phrases 92% of the time at +6 dB and 58% at 0 dB — "Good boy." (confidence 0.82), "Thank you."
   (0.83), "Hey." (0.95), "See you around.", "Oh my god!". A sound with a voice's cadence and no words is exactly what an
   EVP candidate is, so this is the failure that matters.
3. **Whisper's confidence cannot be the safeguard.** At a 0.8 threshold 17% of reversed clips still showed words, while
   only 3% of real voices were shown and right. Confidence hides real voices faster than it hides invented ones.
4. **Whisper stops understanding voices at about the level of the noise.** Right: 50% at +6 dB, 8% at 0 dB, 0% at −6
   and below (short single phrases, 1.2–1.9 s clips). Today's detector on High still *finds* 8 of 12 voices at −6 dB and
   2 of 12 at −12. So in the faint range where EVPs are claimed, Whisper cannot supply the words.
5. **As a voice/not-voice filter, Whisper is promising but loses the faint ones.** On the High queue it wrote words for
   21 of 39 real voices and for none of 350 non-voices — the queue would shrink from 389 to 21, but the 18 voices it
   dropped are the quiet ones an investigator most wants.
6. **Cost is not the obstacle.** 0.66 s per clip, 0.38 s per second of audio on an M-series CPU; a Windows server will be
   slower but a queue of a few hundred clips is minutes, not hours.

## What it means for the design (backlog 242)

- Word suggestions from Whisper are only defensible on loud candidates, always behind blind review, and never filtered
  by its confidence alone. Showing "what it might say" on a faint candidate would mostly show invention.
- The valuable job is **"does this contain a voice at all"** — and it has to keep the faint voices. Whisper is a weak
  fit for that; a purpose-built voice-activity detector (Silero VAD, ~2 MB, not yet approved for download) is the
  obvious next thing to grade on the same corpus, and the reversed-speech control is the test it must pass.
- Every detector result shown to investigators should come with this bench's numbers for its level band.

## Limits of this run

Text-to-speech voices (cleaner and steadier than people), synthesised backgrounds, one seed, short clips with no
surrounding context, English forced. Each is a knob for the next run, not a reason to doubt the direction.
