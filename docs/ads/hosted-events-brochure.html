How it works

Five people.
One unforgettable weekend.

Every hosted event has the same cast. IsHaunted gives each of them exactly the screen they need, and nothing they don't.

Finds it, asks, gets a pass

The guest

Browses the event with its photographs and access notes, asks for a room or picks seats, and keeps a QR pass on the web and the iPhone.

Runs the weekend

The organizer

Confirms parties into rooms, writes to everybody at once, invites helpers, and sees the numbers at a glance.

Lets people in

The helper on the door

Accepts an invitation that grants the door and nothing else, then checks parties in by scan or by name.

Says who hosts there

The venue

Shows the building's history and photographs, and approves the organizers who want to run nights under its roof.

Keeps it trustworthy

IsHaunted

Watches every event on one dashboard, removes what breaks the rules, returns the credit, and hears appeals.

Seats and strangers

Two hundred seats.
No account needed.

For an evening in the ballroom, guests pick their own seats on your plan. Somebody without an account picks too, and we email them a link to hold the seats.

1

Pick on the plan

Sections and prices in a legend; taken and held seats cannot be picked.

2

On a phone, too

The plan scrolls in its own frame with a summary that stays in view.

3

A hold that lapses by itself

Picked seats are held for the venue's chosen time, shown to everybody else as pending, and let go if nobody confirms.
