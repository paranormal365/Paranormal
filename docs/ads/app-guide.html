02 · Field Kit
The phone as an instrument

A meter that knows the room.

Field Kit records an investigation on the device. It needs no signal and no account: start in a cellar with no bars, and nothing leaves the phone until you send it.

Measured against the room

Set a base once the room has settled. The needle then shows how far the magnetic field has moved from it, in milligauss.

Sound, alongside

A sound meter in decibels with your base and report levels marked on it, recording as it goes.

Say which room you're in

One tap names the room; every reading, mark and photo after it belongs there. Nothing else can tell rooms apart.

Where, honestly

Your position, with how accurate it is. Indoors that is often the whole building, so it always says so.

No signal needed

Record all night underground. It keeps everything on the phone until you choose to send it.

A magnetic field meter, not an EMF meter. The phone's magnetometer reads the steady magnetic field — the Earth's, and whatever wiring and iron do to it. It cannot see the AC fields a K-II style detector responds to, and the app says so on the screen.

03 · During the night
Mark it, ask it, leave it

Every moment, found again.

A week later, reviewing is jumping to a list of moments — not scrubbing hours of tape.

Mark and Note

Mark drops a marker at this second. Set a report level and it marks the field or the sound for you, once per event.

Notes you can speak

Spoken notes turn into text on the phone itself — nothing sent anywhere, and it works with no signal.

EVP question and answer

Marks the question and the silence after it, each pointing into the recording, so you know where to listen.

Leave it watching

Set it down to watch the field, the sound, the phone being moved, or movement in the camera's view.

Black out the screen

One tap takes the screen to black and keeps the phone awake. Tap anywhere to bring it back.

Put it in your pocket

With sound on, a session keeps recording when the phone is locked. The review shows any gap.

04 · Afterward
Play it back, send it on

The night, on one playhead.

The trace, the map, the compass, the sound and the video move together, with every mark listed to jump to.

Review on the phone

Marks land a few seconds before the event, so you hear what led up to it. Video plays with the session's sound.

Photos in the strip

Each photo glows as playback passes the moment it was taken. Tap one to see it full size.

Straight to the case

Send a session to your group's investigation when there is signal; it plays on the website exactly as here.

Send only what mattered

Trim to the part that counts before it goes, instead of a whole night of quiet.

Public places, in the open

Recorded somewhere public? Publish it to the place's archive, and play what others found there.

Hand it to anyone

Export the session as a file. Another IsHaunted phone opens it and plays it, marked as somebody else's.

05 · Nights out
Ghost walks, events and the door

Book it. Show up. Get in.

Tours and events near you, readable without an account — and everything for the night itself in your pocket.

Ghost walks near you

How far away they are and when they next run. Location is asked for, never taken; type a city instead if you like.

Events, on your clock

Times read where you are, with the zone beside them. Add a night to your own calendar in one tap.

A pass with no signal

Your pass, the program and the menus are saved on the phone, so they open in a cellar with no bars.

Photos for the night

Share photos to the event's room; with no signal they wait on the phone and go when you're back in range.

Running the door

Helpers check people in by name or by scanning their pass — with or without a signal.

Start the night together

A lead can start everybody's Field Kit from a push. Nobody is taken over; you join when you're ready.
