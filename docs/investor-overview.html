The opportunity

A passionate market that nobody has built for

Every town has a haunted house, and every region has the people who investigate it. Thousands of volunteer paranormal investigation groups worldwide field requests from frightened homeowners, survey historic sites, and produce photo, audio and video evidence — and today they run all of it on spreadsheets, Facebook groups, and shared drives.

This is exactly the shape of market where vertical platforms win: recurring group activity, sensitive records that deserve real tooling, media-heavy evidence that deserves real processing, a public audience hungry for the findings, and clients — the families who ask for help — who currently get no professional experience at all. No incumbent owns this vertical. IsHaunted.com was built to own it end to end.

The product

One platform, both sides of the story

For investigation groups: their entire operation

Client intake, case management, scheduled investigations, evidence files, team roles and permissions, equipment inventory and lending, calendars and public events, member recruitment, and each group's own public website — authored and published on the platform, no webmaster required. A group that adopts IsHaunted.com moves its whole working life here.

For everyone else: the public stage

Published cases plotted on a world map, a directory of groups searchable by location, public events anyone can attend, long-form write-ups, and a social feed for the paranormal-curious. The public side is the content engine: every case a group publishes becomes a page that recruits the next group, the next member, and the next client.

The differentiator: privacy engineering. A paranormal case is somebody's home. IsHaunted.com treats that seriously in ways no general-purpose tool can: camera GPS data is stripped from served media while originals are preserved as evidence; client names are replaced with pseudonyms on every public page; published locations are generalized to the area, never the address; and the platform detects a client's name in a report before it goes public and warns the author. This is deep, tested machinery — and it is also the paid product (below).
Built-in media production

The evidence pipeline is the product

Paranormal investigation runs on media, so the platform doesn't stop at storing files — it processes them. A browser-based video editor (multi-track timeline, overlays, callouts, keyframes, export) ships inside the product, with an optional native helper app that renders at full speed on the investigator's own machine. Server-side EVP detection scans audio evidence for anomalies automatically, behind an accuracy-gated test suite. Waveform players, spectrograms, clip extraction and multi-track case mixes round out the audio bench.

Nobody else in this vertical ships an editor, a detector, or a provenance trail. A group that cuts its evidence reel here has no reason to leave.

Community

The feed: from audience to members

The community layer is live in the product today: member profiles, @handles, following, hashtags, and a moderated feed. The full front-door arc is now built and dark-launched — anyone can scroll without an account; posting means joining or founding a group; every photo and video is screened on our own servers before it appears; and groups' promoted cards ride the feed, served nearest the viewer first. One switch turns it on.

  • Browser video editor with native helper app for full-speed rendering
  • Server-side EVP detection with an accuracy-gated fixture suite
  • Audio tooling: waveform players, spectrograms, region notes, clip extraction, case mixes
  • Evidence metadata: EXIF/GPS extraction on every upload into an audit table; location stripping from served copies; derived clips inherit the recording's provenance
  • Per-group public websites (CMS): pages, layouts, logos, embedded cases and galleries
  • Equipment: personal and group inventories, a moderated global catalog, lending with condition photos
  • Events: public listings, RSVPs, attendee evidence submission, reminder automation

  • Community: profiles, @handles, following, hashtags, and the full public feed — anonymous scroll, ranked timeline, photo and video posts screened on our own servers before they appear, and a classifier that learns from every moderator decision
  • Group discovery by location, by kind (investigation groups and ghost walking tours), and group-authored promotional ads with platform review, served nearest-viewer-first
  • Native iPhone and iPad app on the same API, running on device today
  • Billing foundation: tiers by member count, contract-terms freezing, coupons, referral tracking, an append-only financial ledger with receipts and per-state tax

Engineering discipline as an asset: 3,270 automated unit tests and a 350+ scenario browser test suite run against every change; an append-only audit trail on financial records; additive-only database migrations; and a documented decision record covering 180+ tracked product items. The codebase is built to be operated, audited, and grown.
Business model

Free where it grows, paid where it matters

The pricing principle: charge for the work that carries responsibility, never for the activity that grows the community.

Free lanePaid lane
Public-place investigations — landmarks, historic sites, businesses — including full public publication of findings. This is the content engine and the recruitment engine, and it stays free. Private-residence and client work — taking a family's case, working it, and publishing redacted findings from it. The privacy machinery (pseudonyms, location generalization, media stripping, name detection) is the product being bought.

Revenue levers, designed and largely built:

  • Subscriptions by group size — tiered bands priced per member count, monthly or yearly, with contract terms frozen per period (a group always keeps what it bought).
  • Overflow seats — when a group outgrows its band, new members can subscribe individually rather than forcing the whole group up a tier. Built; priced per band.
  • Capability gating — per-tier switches over discrete features (case transfers, audio/video location stripping, private-residence casework), each enforced server-side with honest, upgrade-aware messaging in the product.
  • Referral program — per-referrer coupon campaigns with frozen-money attribution and a commission ledger; referral links land visitors on pre-discounted pricing.
  • Group promotion — groups author ads, the platform reviews them, and placements run across the site; location-targeted feed placement is on the roadmap below.
