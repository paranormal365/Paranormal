The opportunity

A real community with no real infrastructure

Paranormal investigation is a durable, passionate niche: thousands of volunteer groups worldwide field requests from homeowners, investigate historic sites, and produce photo, audio and video evidence — and they run it all on spreadsheets, Facebook groups, and shared drives.

The work has everything a vertical platform is built for: recurring group activity, sensitive records that deserve real tooling, media-heavy evidence that deserves real processing, a public audience hungry for the findings, and clients — the families who ask for help — who currently get no professional experience at all. No incumbent owns this vertical. IsHaunted.com is built to.

The product

Two sides, one platform

For investigation groups: a complete operations platform

Everything a group runs, in one place: client intake, case management, scheduled investigations, evidence files, team roles and permissions, equipment inventory and lending, calendars and public events, member recruitment, and each group's own public website — pages they author and publish under the platform, without needing a webmaster.

For everyone else: the public side

Published cases and findings, a directory of groups searchable by location, public events anyone can attend, long-form publications, and a social feed for the paranormal-interested — built, and launching as the engagement centerpiece (see Upcoming Features).

The differentiator: privacy engineering. A paranormal case is somebody's home. IsHaunted.com treats that seriously in ways no general-purpose tool can: camera GPS data is stripped from served media while originals are preserved as evidence; client names are replaced with pseudonyms on every public page; published locations are generalized to the area, never the address; and the platform detects a client's name in a report before it goes public and warns the author. This is deep, tested machinery — and it is also the paid product (below).
What exists today

Built, tested, and deployed

The platform is live at ishaunted.com. This is not a prototype — it is a working product with production-grade engineering discipline:

  • Full case lifecycle: intake requests, case records, timelines, investigations, findings, reports
  • A browser-based video editor (overlays, keyframes, callouts, audio tools) with an optional native helper app for fast, full-quality rendering on the investigator's own machine
  • Server-side EVP detection — automated scanning of audio evidence for anomalies, with an accuracy-gated test fixture suite
  • Audio tooling: waveform players, spectrograms, region notes, clip extraction, multi-track case mixes
  • Evidence metadata: EXIF/GPS extraction on every upload into an audit table; location stripping from served copies; derived clips inherit the recording's provenance
  • Per-group public websites (CMS): pages, layouts, logos, embedded cases and galleries
  • Equipment: personal and group inventories, a moderated global catalog, lending with condition photos
  • Events: public listings, RSVPs, attendee evidence submission, reminder automation
  • Roles & permissions: per-group custom roles with per-table grants, tier-aware
  • Community: member profiles, @handles, following, hashtags, a moderated social feed (built, dark-launched)
  • Group discovery by location; group-authored promotional ads with platform review
  • Billing foundation: subscription tiers by member count, contract-terms freezing, coupons and referral tracking, an append-only financial ledger with receipts and per-state tax
  • In-app help system with full product documentation, generated to PDF
Engineering discipline as an asset: 3,000+ automated unit tests and a 350+ scenario browser test suite run against every change; an append-only audit trail on financial records; additive-only database migrations; and a documented decision record covering 180+ tracked product items. The codebase is built to be operated, audited, and grown.
Business model

Free where it grows, paid where it matters

The pricing principle: charge for the work that carries responsibility, never for the activity that grows the community.

Free lanePaid lane
Public-place investigations — landmarks, historic sites, businesses — including full public publication of findings. This is the content engine and the recruitment engine, and it stays free. Private-residence and client work — taking a family's case, working it, and publishing redacted findings from it. The privacy machinery (pseudonyms, location generalization, media stripping, name detection) is the product being bought.

Revenue levers, designed and largely built:

  • Subscriptions by group size — tiered bands priced per member count, monthly or yearly, with contract terms frozen per period (a group always keeps what it bought).
  • Overflow seats — when a group outgrows its band, new members can subscribe individually rather than forcing the whole group up a tier. Built; priced per band.
  • Capability gating — per-tier switches over discrete features (case transfers between groups, audio/video location stripping, private-residence casework), each enforced server-side with honest, upgrade-aware messaging in the product.
  • Referral program — per-referrer coupon campaigns with frozen-money attribution and a commission ledger; referral links land visitors on pre-discounted pricing.
  • Group promotion — groups author ads, the platform reviews them, and placements run across the site; location-targeted feed placement is on the roadmap below.
