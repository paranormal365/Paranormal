**Publications** are where groups write at length. A publication is a group's own title — *Field
Notes*, *The Ridgeway Case*, whatever they call it — and inside it are posts: case write-ups,
research, notes from a night that turned into something worth explaining properly.

If [the Feed](/help/the-feed) is what somebody says on the way home, a publication is what they
write the following week.

## You do not need an account

Everything published is readable by anyone. No sign-in, no email address, nothing to dismiss
before you can read. Share a link to a post and the person you send it to can simply read it.

An account only matters if you want to **subscribe**, which is how you keep track of the groups
whose writing you want to keep up with.

*Every group with something published. The card shows how many posts there are and when the most

## Finding something to read

**Publications** in the left-hand menu — under **Community** once you are signed in — lists every
publication with at least one published post.
Publications with nothing in them yet are not listed — a title that leads to an empty page is not
worth the click.

Open one and you get its posts, newest first, each with a line or two about what it covers.

![A publication and its posts](/help/media/publications/publication.png)
*A publication's own page. The group's name links back to its profile, and **Subscribe** is in the
top right.*

## Subscribing

**Subscribe** on a publication's page tells the group you are reading. If you are not signed in
the button says **Sign in to subscribe** instead, and brings you back to the same page afterwards.

Your subscriptions appear at the top of the **Publications** page, with the date of each one's
most recent post, so you can see at a glance who has written something new.

**Subscribed** on a publication you already follow unsubscribes you. Nothing is lost by
unsubscribing — you can subscribe again later, and everything published stays readable either way.

## Posts marked "Subscribers"

Some posts may be marked **Subscribers**. You will see the title and the opening lines, and the
rest is only for people who subscribe.

Today nothing on the site is actually marked this way — every post is free to read in full. The
label exists because groups have asked about it, and it is better for you to have seen it
explained here than to meet it for the first time on a post you wanted to read.

## Reading a post

A post is a page: a title, the date it was published, and the piece itself. There is nothing to
expand, no "continue reading", and no interruption partway through.

If a link you followed leads nowhere, the post has been withdrawn. Groups can take a post down —
usually because something in it was wrong — and a withdrawn post stops being served straight away.
