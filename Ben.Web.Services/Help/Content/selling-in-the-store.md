If you make equipment and sell it through the store, you have a **Selling** section in the menu.
It holds everything to do with your own items, and only yours: you never see anybody else's, and
the store's administration stays with its administrators.

## Your items

**Selling → My Items** lists every item the store sells for you, with:

- **Status** — *Draft* (not on sale yet), *On sale*, or *Off sale* (on sale once, not now).
- **Price** — what the store sells it for. The store sets each item's price, so it's shown here
  but can't be changed from here; get in touch if it should be different.
- **In stock**, **Sold** and **Last sold** — how many are on the shelf, how many have sold, and
  when the last one went.

The counts at the top say how many of your items are drafts, on sale and off sale.
