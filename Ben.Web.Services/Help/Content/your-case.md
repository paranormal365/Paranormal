## The case page

![The client view of a case](/help/media/your-case/my-cases.png)
*My Cases lists the cases a group has opened for you.*

**My Cases** lists every case you are part of, whether you opened it or were invited onto it by
someone else. Opening one shows the calendar, everything logged so far, files, and your message
thread with the investigators.

## Logging an occurrence

Use **Log Occurrence** whenever something happens. Record it while it is fresh — the date and time
matter more than polish. You can attach photos, audio, or video, and you can tag what kind of
experience it was so investigators can group similar reports.

Several people can report the same moment. That is expected, not a problem: the timeline shows
entries that share a time together and labels them, because two independent accounts of one event
are more useful than one.

## Who sees what

| Marked | Seen by |
|---|---|
| Internal | The investigators only. |
| Shared with you | The investigators and you. |
| Public | Anyone, if the case is ever published. |

Your own reports are always visible to you and to the group working your case.

## How you are named publicly

If a case is ever published, you choose the name shown — "A local family", "The homeowner", or
anything you prefer. Set it on your case page under **How You're Named Publicly**.

Your investigators always see your real name. They cannot work a case while blind to who reported
it, so the alias applies to public pages and shared documents only.
